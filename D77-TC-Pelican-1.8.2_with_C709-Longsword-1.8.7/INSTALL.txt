D77-TC Pelican 1.8.2 (with C709 Longsword 1.8.7)

Requires: Nuclear Option 0.34.6 with BepInEx 5 (tested with BepInEx 5.4.23.5).
Optional: Blueprinter 2.x. Both plugins work with or without it.

Install
1. Close Nuclear Option.
2. Replace any existing D77TCPelican and C709Longsword folders in:
   <Nuclear Option>\BepInEx\plugins\
   with the two folders from this package.
3. Start the game. BepInEx\LogOutput.log should show:
   Loading [C709 Longsword 1.8.7]
   Loading [D77-TC Pelican 1.8.2]
4. The Pelican appears in the mission editor aircraft catalog as "D77-TC Pelican".

Both folders are required. The Pelican uses the Longsword's flight model and
will not load without it. Keep each folder's Airframe folder, and the Pelican's
Audio, Pilot and Troops folders, beside its DLL. The Longsword's Pilot folder
is also required.

Multiplayer: every player needs the same two folders, with the same versions.

Pelican controls
- Custom Axis 1 vectors the nozzles from 0 to 90 degrees.
- Hold Brake with the throttle above idle to deploy reverse thrust. At idle,
  Brake is the wheel brake.
- In VTOL with the nozzles at least 80 degrees down, holding Brake captures the
  altitude. Release Brake, move the throttle, or vector out to take over.

Uninstall: close the game and delete the two folders from BepInEx\plugins.
